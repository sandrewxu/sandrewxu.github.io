I am a third-year undergraduate at Yale pursuing a B.S./M.S. in Computer Science and B.S. in Mathematics on the Intensive Track. I am technically interested in building the next generation of AI and intellectually interested in how it can benefit society. I have taken advanced coursework in natural language processing, deep learning, statistical and probabilistic machine learning, programming, and mathematics and am always open to interesting opportunities.

A copy of my resume is available [here](files/Andrew_Xu_Resume_05282025.pdf).

### Research

Currently, I am designing LLM-based agents for scientific innovation and paper writing in the [Yale NLP Lab](https://nlp.cs.yale.edu/) with [Dr. Arman Cohan](https://armancohan.com/). I am also implementing an agentic system for LLMs to automate complex physics proof generation under Dr. John Sous.

Previously, I did research on algorthmic fairness with [Dr. Nisheeth Vishnoi](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/vishnoi/Home.html) and published a [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04897) at ICML 2024.
