### Research

Currently, I am developing retrievers for reasoning-intensive tasks in the [Yale NLP Lab](https://nlp.cs.yale.edu/) with [Dr. Arman Cohan](https://armancohan.com/) and [Dr. Rex Ying](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/ying-rex/).

Previously, I did research on algorthmic fairness with [Dr. Nisheeth Vishnoi](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/vishnoi/Home.html) and published a [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04897) at ICML 2024.
